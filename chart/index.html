Advisories

  • Greywatch Station. Concordat customs. Every ship on the Kestrel–Solenne run is inspected.
  • Skeinstone Belt. Contested. Salvage claims are disputed; Vantage patrols.
  • The Cinderfield. Wreck-strewn and lawless. Brasswater Bazaar trades there.
  • Corvus, Vantic system. No scheduled traffic. Ferrylight carries no listings for Corvus.
  • Off the charted threads. Travel off-route isn't charted. Ferrylight can't help you there.
