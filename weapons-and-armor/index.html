Concealing magic: Quiet gear

These tools make a caster harder to catch. None of them is magic (it is lining, ash and clever instruments), so none signals. If a Registry inspector finds one, it counts as evidence of intent. The Compact will find you a seller, at the listed price.

Each item has a rating (0 for cantrips, up to 3) and charges that refresh at dawn. Cast a spell at or below the rating and a charge is spent: the casting is muffled. A muffled spell is not detected in Tier 1 or 2 warden coverage unless a lamp is within 10 ft, and in Tier 3 it counts as a cantrip (detected within 30 ft of a lamp) instead of instantly. A spell above the rating is detected as normal and spends no charge. A muffled concentration spell is picked up by the hourly check only on a 1. Quiet gear hides your casting, and nothing else.
