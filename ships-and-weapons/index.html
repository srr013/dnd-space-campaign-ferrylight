Ships and Weapons

This sheet lists the ships and ship weapons you can normally buy, and the rules for using them in a fight. Prices are in marks. Unique ships and rare weapons are not on it. The weapon table on the Ship weapons section shows where each class behaves differently.

Haulers carry the most and fight the least: Light mounts only, thin shields, slow engines. They cannot outrun a warship, so they travel with escorts or run early. Every ship has the same three stations (Pilot, Gunner, Engineer); extra crew on bigger ships let one Gunner run several mounts. A Heavy mount (the Lance) counts as two mounts. A cargo expansion (about 400 marks, once per ship, not for Skiffs) adds 50% to the hold, and a ton is a crate-stack a small crew can move in an afternoon.

Space combat basics

One square is 10 km. Count squares between the two ships; a diagonal counts as 1. Boarding is not a range: hooks and Rendezvous need the two ships in adjacent squares.

Two ships flying at each other close by the sum of their moves. A ship running straight away from an equal-Speed pursuer holds the range steady; the faster ship gains the difference each round. Every Engine point is a whole square, so the Engineer's power split decides most chases.

No system takes more than 3 points, and each row includes everything above it. The class Shield bonus is added to the first level. Shields regenerate at the start of your own turn, before the Engineer acts, at the level you began the turn with. The second point is a 15-shield layer: raise Shields to 2 and you gain 15 shields at once; drop below 2 and you lose 15 at once (minimum 0), however much damage you took in between. It is a buffer for the round you expect to be hit, not a lasting gain. The third point adds no shields: it gives +3 AC against every ship weapon, missiles and mines included, while Shields stay at 3.

Kinetic weapons and the +2 AC

A ship that moved on its own most recent turn has +2 AC against kinetic weapons. Direction does not matter: toward the shooter, away from it or sideways all count, as long as it moved at least 1 square. Holding still, only pivoting, or being pushed by terrain gives no bonus. Beams and missiles ignore movement.

A ship's round

  1. Start of turn: shields regenerate at the level the ship began the turn with.
  2. Engineer acts next: Reroute Power (up to 2 points, no roll), Overcharge (DC 15), Repair (DC 12) or Spin Up (DC 12). One action.
  3. Pilot pivots, then moves. One maneuver, one roll. A failed Cruise or Hard Turn moves 1 square less; Back Off is always 1 square.
  4. Gunner fires each mount once, at any moment of the turn: before the Pilot pivots, between any two squares of the move, or after it. Range and line of fire are measured from where the ship is when the shot goes. The Gunner may split mounts between targets.
  5. Reactions (Shoot Down, Flare, Juke) can happen at any point. At the end of the turn a ship in a debris ring checks for damage; at the end of the round, currents and gravity move ships (see Space terrain).

At Hull 0 a ship is Disabled: Engines and Weapons go offline and cannot be repaired in that fight, and shields are lost. Crew can still Transmit and abandon ship. The next hit that damages its Hull destroys it, unless the attacker chooses only to disable it.

Escaping a system: Thread Mouths

There is no system edge and no gate. The Skein is a set of fixed lanes, and each charted route has a Thread Mouth in every system it joins: a 3x3 patch of space. You can only enter the Skein there.

To jump you must accompliash all three in the same roundDetails
1. Engines 2 or moreEngines must be online with at least 2 points when the Engineer acts. You do not need all 3 points: a Cutter keeps 1 point for Shields or Weapons. Offline Engines, a Disabled ship or a Hooked ship cannot jump.
2. Engineer: Spin Up (DC 12)Tinker's Tools or Arcana. Success: the drive is seated. Failure: nothing happens. It uses the Engineer's action, so set the power split the round before.
3. Pilot ends on the MouthAny Mouth square. If Spin Up worked, the ship jumps at the end of its own turn, before any enemy acts. If the Pilot falls short (a failed Cruise moves 1 square less), the Spin Up is wasted and the Engineer rolls again next round.

The run: a Mouth is about 6 squares away in a shipping lane and about 10 near a world. A Cutter at Engines 2 covers 10 squares in 5 rounds; at Engines 3 in 4, with nothing left for defense. Called Shot on Engines or Engine Cut slows the run, and a boarding Hook stops it. Pursuit: an enemy that reaches the Mouth and succeeds at its own Spin Up the round after you jump may follow, arriving 1d4 hours behind you; later than that, the wake is lost. In the thread nothing can fight, and the ship travels the route's legs (about a day each). Everyone aboard and all cargo leave with the ship, so anyone left behind is stranded.

Space terrain

Space near anything worth fighting over is not empty. A planet is never a token: at 10 km a square a world is hundreds of squares across, so it is drawn as a curved limb along one edge of the board, with its atmosphere and low-orbit rows in front of it. The 3x3 rock is a moonlet, about 30 km across. Being moved by terrain is never moving on your own: no +2 AC against kinetics.

Offline systems and add-ons

Unpowered means the Engineer put 0 points in a system; nothing is broken and a Reroute fixes it. Offline means the system is damaged and gives no benefit at all, whatever points sit in it; only a Repair (Engineer, DC 12, the whole action) or the effect running out fixes it. Ten quiet minutes between fights repairs everything, no roll.

SystemWhile offline
EnginesSpeed 0: no Cruise, Hard Turn, About-Face, Back Off, Rendezvous or Afterburn. The ship can still pivot up to 90 degrees free, no roll. Pilot checks have disadvantage, and a ship that did not move gets no +2 AC against kinetics. Close In, Slingshot and Overburn do not work. On the board this is the same as 0 points, but a Reroute can't fix it and the ship can't Spin Up.
ShieldsCapacity 0, no regen, current shields lost. Shield Surge and Capacitor Bank restores do not work (an unpowered Shields system can still be restored by Shield Surge; an offline one cannot).
WeaponsNo weapon fires. A charging or held Lance loses its charge. Shoot Down, Point Defense and every attacking Doctrine ability are unavailable. Flares and Transmit still work.
One mountOnly that mount cannot fire; a Lance in it loses its charge.
A Core pointThe Power Core is 1 lower until repaired. If more points are allocated than the core has, re-split them immediately, free.
