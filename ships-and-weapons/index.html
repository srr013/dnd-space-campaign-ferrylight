Ships and Weapons

Haulers carry the most and fight the least: Light mounts only, thin shields, slow engines. They cannot outrun a warship, so they travel with escorts or run early. Every ship has the same three stations (Pilot, Gunner, Engineer); extra crew on bigger ships let one Gunner run several mounts. A Heavy mount (the Lance) counts as two mounts. A cargo expansion (about 400 marks, once per ship, not for Skiffs) adds 50% to the hold, and a ton is a crate-stack a small crew can move in an afternoon.

Space combat basics

One square is 10 km. Count squares between the two ships; a diagonal counts as 1. Boarding is not a range: hooks and Rendezvous need the two ships in adjacent squares.

No system takes more than 3 points, and each row includes everything above it. The class Shield bonus is added whenever Shields has at least 1 point. Shields regenerate at the start of your own turn.

Kinetic weapons and the +2 AC

A ship that moved on its own most recent turn has +2 AC against kinetic weapons. Direction does not matter: toward the shooter, away from it or sideways all count, as long as it moved at least 1 square. Holding still, or only pivoting, gives no bonus. Beams and missiles ignore movement.

A ship's round

  1. Engineer acts first: Reroute Power (up to 2 points, no roll), Overcharge (DC 15), Repair (DC 12) or Spin Up (DC 12). One action.
  2. Pilot pivots, then moves. One maneuver, one roll.
  3. Gunner fires. Each mount makes one attack per round, and the Gunner may split mounts between targets.
  4. Reactions (Shoot Down, Flare, Juke) can happen at any point.

At Hull 0 a ship is Disabled: Engines and Weapons go offline and cannot be repaired in that fight, and shields are lost. Crew can still Transmit and abandon ship. The next hit that damages its Hull destroys it, unless the attacker chooses only to disable it.

Escaping a system: Thread Mouths

There is no system edge and no gate. The Skein is a set of fixed lanes, and each charted route has a Thread Mouth in every system it joins: a 3x3 patch of space. You can only enter the Skein there.

To jump you must accompliash all three in the same roundDetails
1. Engines 2 or moreEngines must be online with at least 2 points when the Engineer acts. You do not need all 3 points: a Cutter keeps 1 point for Shields or Weapons. Offline Engines, a Disabled ship or a Hooked ship cannot jump.
2. Engineer: Spin Up (DC 12)Tinker's Tools or Arcana. Success: the drive is seated. Failure: nothing happens. It uses the Engineer's action, so set the power split the round before.
3. Pilot ends on the MouthAny Mouth square. If Spin Up worked, the ship jumps at the end of its own turn, before any enemy acts. If the Pilot falls short (a failed Cruise halves the move), the Spin Up is wasted and the Engineer rolls again next round.

The run: a Cutter at Engines 2 covers 30 squares in 5 rounds; at Engines 3 in 4, with nothing left for defense. Called Shot on Engines or Engine Cut slows the run, and a boarding Hook stops it. Pursuit: an enemy that reaches the Mouth and succeeds at its own Spin Up the round after you jump may follow, arriving 1d4 hours behind you; later than that, the wake is lost. In the thread nothing can fight, and the ship travels the route's legs (about a day each). Everyone aboard and all cargo leave with the ship, so anyone left behind is stranded.

The Lance, step by step

RoundWhat happens
1. LockThe Gunner names a target in range and line of fire. The mount is locked and the glow is visible to everyone.
2. ChargeThe lock holds even if the target moves out of arc. Changing targets restarts the charge at round 1.
3. FireAttack the locked ship, which must be legal now (range, line of fire, not silent, not hidden by Dark Burst). Hit: 10d10. Miss: the Lance charges again. Target not legal: nothing fires and the Lance stays held, ready for any later round.
LostThe charge is lost if Weapons goes unpowered or offline, the mount is knocked offline (Mount Breaker), or the Gunner re-aims.

Offline systems and add-ons

Unpowered means the Engineer put 0 points in a system; nothing is broken and a Reroute fixes it. Offline means the system is damaged and gives no benefit at all, whatever points sit in it; only a Repair (Engineer, DC 12, the whole action) or the effect running out fixes it. Ten quiet minutes between fights repairs everything, no roll.

SystemWhile offline
EnginesSpeed 0: no Cruise, Hard Turn, About-Face, Back Off, Rendezvous or Afterburn. The ship can still pivot up to 90 degrees free, no roll. Pilot checks have disadvantage, and a ship that did not move gets no +2 AC against kinetics. Close In, Slingshot and Overburn do not work. This is worse than 0 points, which still gives Speed 2.
ShieldsCapacity 0, no regen, current shields lost. Shield Surge and Capacitor Bank restores do not work (an unpowered Shields system can still be restored by Shield Surge; an offline one cannot).
WeaponsNo weapon fires. A charging or held Lance loses its charge. Shoot Down, Point Defense and every attacking Doctrine ability are unavailable. Flares and Transmit still work.
One mountOnly that mount cannot fire; a Lance in it loses its charge.
A Core pointThe Power Core is 1 lower until repaired. If more points are allocated than the core has, re-split them immediately, free.
