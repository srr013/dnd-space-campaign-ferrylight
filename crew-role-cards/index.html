Pilot

You fly the ship. Every turn is pivot, then move, never the other way round: pick one maneuver, make one roll (Vehicles (Space), DEX or INT, + proficiency with Piloting training). Ship initiative is d20 + your DEX (+ proficiency with Piloting). No momentum: the ship stops where you leave it.

Facing

Eight facings. Up to 90° is free with any maneuver. 135° is a Hard Turn, 180° an About-Face. Facing only decides which way you move: the guns turn all the way round.

Rules you steer by

  • Evasion. If you moved at least 1 square on your own last turn, kinetic weapons (railguns) are at +2 AC against you. Beams and missiles ignore it. Being pushed by terrain doesn't count.
  • Closing and fleeing. Range changes by the difference in moves. Equal Speeds can't shake each other by running: you need terrain, a better turn, more Engine points, or a Mouth.
  • Breaking contact. End two rounds in a row at 21+ squares from every pursuer.
  • Thread Mouth (3×3). If the Engineer's Spin Up worked this turn and you end your move on any Mouth square, the ship jumps at the end of its turn.

Terrain and you

  • Planet limb: blocks every shot, missiles too. Put the world between you and them.
  • Low orbit (3 rows off the limb): end of round, pulled 1 square toward the planet (2 at Engines 0).
  • Debris ring: Advance at least 1 square with the flow arrow (within 45°) every turn you end inside it, or take 2d6 hull.
  • Tidal current: end of round it carries you 1 square; Advance with it for +1 square.
  • Ion cloud: inside it no one targets beyond Close, in or out.
  • Mine picket: each round you enter a mined square, +5 vs your AC, 2d6. Juke works on mines.

Talk to your crew

  • Engineer, before they split power: "I need N squares this turn." Every Engine point is a square now.
  • Gunner, before you move: call your path, square by square. They can shoot from any square on it, so ask "Where do you want to stop and fire?"
  • Railgun enemy? Keep moving (+2 AC) and stay 7 squares or more away: it can't reach past 6.
Any seat, any ship. Transmit (bonus action): send a burst message: alert, location ping, distress call. It can't be jammed. Abandon ship (action): into the boat or a void-suit. Gunner

You fire the ship's weapons: d20 + DEX, plus your proficiency bonus if you have Gunnery training. Weapons sit in turrets that turn all the way round, so you can hit anything in range and in the clear, in any direction. Each weapon fires once a round, at any moment of your ship's turn: before the Pilot pivots, between any two squares of the move, or at the end. Measure range and line of fire from wherever the ship is when you pull the trigger. A miss never hurts you.

Core: Skiff 1, Cutter 3, Sloop 4, bigger ships 4-8. Never more than 3 in one system. Shield capacity adds the ship's Shield bonus (Cutter +0, Sloop +15).

Shields: the 15-point layer

  • Raise Shields to 2: gain 15 shields now. Drop below 2: lose 15 shields now (minimum 0), however much damage you took meanwhile. A second point is a buffer for the round you expect to be hit.
  • Shields 3 adds no shields: +3 AC against every ship weapon, missiles and mines included, while it stays at 3. Stacks with the Pilot's +2 vs kinetics.
  • Regeneration comes at the start of your turn, before you act, at the level you set last turn. Raising Shields this turn doesn't regenerate more until next turn.
  • An Overcharge or Redline to Shields 2 gives the 15 for that round only; it goes when the extra point does.

Unpowered, offline, disabled

  • Unpowered: you put 0 points there. A Reroute fixes it.
  • Offline: damaged. No benefit whatever points it has, until a Repair or the effect's duration ends. Offline Engines = Speed 0 and no Spin Up. Offline Shields = shields lost. Offline Weapons = nothing fires.
  • Disabled: Hull 0. Engines and Weapons out for the fight, shields gone. You can still Transmit and abandon ship.
  • Between fights, ten quiet minutes repairs everything offline, no roll.

A Mock Flight

Three rounds of a laser cutter against a railgun raider, so you can see a whole turn of ship combat. Every roll uses the real DCs and every range and line of fire is to scale, so you can check any line against your cards.

0. Start of turnshields regenerate at the level you begin the turn with
1. Engineersets the power; Shields changes apply at once
2. Pilot + GunnerPilot pivots, then moves. Gunner fires at any moment: before, between squares, after
3. End of turndebris ring check
End of roundcurrents and gravity move ships

You (blue): a Cutter. Power Core 3, split Engines 1 / Shields 1 / Weapons 1: Speed 1, shield capacity 20. Hull 50, AC 12. One Pulse Laser (2d6 + 2, reaches 12 squares). Your Pilot has Piloting (+5); your Gunner has Gunnery (+5 to hit).

Raider (red): the same hull with a Railgun (3d8, reaches only 6 squares). Its Pilot rolls +3; its Gunner +4.

Start: you at (4, 8) facing east, shields 20. Raider at (14, 9) facing west, shields 20, 10 squares away (Medium). An asteroid sits at (7, 8). You act first. Shields regenerate at the start of a ship's own turn if powered.

1 square = 10 km. Solid triangle = the ship now, pointing the way it faces (blue filled = you, outlined = raider). Faint triangle = where it started the round. Solid arrow = the move. Dashed line = a shot; a yellow dot marks a shot fired partway through the move.

Round 1: Fire from the first square Shields into Engines; shooting mid-move past the rock

EngineerThe raider carries a railgun, and it is 10 squares off: a railgun can't reach past 6. So Reroute Power: 1 point from Shields to Engines. Engines 2, Shields 0, Weapons 1. Speed 2, advantage on Pilot rolls, and no shields for now. No roll.
PilotCruise (DC 8), with advantage. Pivot: 45° to face north-east (free). Move: Advance 2 squares, (4, 8) to (5, 9) to (6, 10). Rolls 11 and 7, takes 11: 11 + 5 = 16. Success.
GunnerFrom the start square the asteroid sits right on the line. The Gunner calls "after one square": at (5, 9) the line is clear, 9 squares, Medium, inside the laser's 12. d20 + 5: rolls 14, total 19 against AC 12. Hit. 2d6 + 2 = 4 + 3 + 2 = 9. Raider shields 20 → 11. The Pilot finishes the move.
RaiderShields regenerate +5 (11 → 16). Engines 1, so Speed 1. Pilot: Cruise west, 1 square to (13, 9); roll 9 + 3 = 12, success. Gunner: you are 7 squares away, Medium. The railgun reaches Close (1-6) only. No shot.

End of round. You: (6, 10) facing NE, shields 0, hull 50. Raider: (13, 9) facing W, shields 16, hull 50.

Why it worked. A railgun crew has to come to you. Outside 6 squares your shields were doing nothing, so the Engineer spent them on a square of Speed.

Round 2: Back off and shoot Holding the range; the raider gambles to close

EngineerNo change: Engines 2, Shields 0, Weapons 1. Your shields stay down (unpowered, no regeneration).
PilotKeep the range: Back Off (DC 13), with advantage. Pivot: none, nose kept north-east. Move: back 1 square to (5, 9). Rolls 12 and 15: 15 + 5 = 20. Success.
GunnerFire after the move: raider at (13, 9), 8 squares, Medium, clear. d20 + 5: rolls 6, total 11 against AC 12. Miss. Nothing else happens.
RaiderShields regenerate to 20. Then its Engineer gambles: Reroute Shields to Engines (Engines 2, Shields 0, Weapons 1). Its shields drop to 0 at once. Pilot: Cruise west 2 squares to (11, 9), with advantage; 10 + 3 = 13, success. Gunner fires at the end of the move: 6 squares, Close, clear. You moved last turn, so the railgun needs AC 12 + 2 = 14. d20 + 4: rolls 13, total 17. Hit. 3d8 = 5 + 4 + 4 = 13. No shields to stop it: your hull 50 → 37.

End of round. You: (5, 9) facing NE, shields 0, hull 37. Raider: (11, 9) facing W, shields 0, hull 50.

The cost. Running shields-down is fine while their gun can't reach you. Once they reach 6 squares, every hit is on the hull. Back Off holds range by 1, but a Speed 2 pursuer gains 1 a round.

Round 3: Shoot, then duck Fire before the move, then put the rock in the way

EngineerNo regeneration (Shields were at 0 when your turn began). Reroute 1 point from Engines to Shields: Engines 1, Shields 1, Weapons 1. Capacity 20, but they refill only at the start of your next turn. Speed 1.
PilotCruise (DC 8). Pivot: 90°, north-east to south-east (free). Move: Advance 1 square to (6, 8), with the asteroid between you and the raider. Roll 8 + 5 = 13. Success.
GunnerFire before the Pilot moves, while you still have a line: from (5, 9) the raider at (11, 9) is 6 squares, Close, clear. d20 + 5: rolls 15, total 20. Hit. 2d6 + 2 = 5 + 6 + 2 = 13. It has no shields: raider hull 50 → 37. Then the Pilot ducks behind the rock.
RaiderShields at 0, no regeneration. Pilot: Cruise west 2 squares, (11, 9) to (10, 9) to (9, 9), with advantage; 12 + 3 = 15, success. The Gunner looks for a line from every square of the move: the asteroid is in the way from all three. No shot.

End of round. You: (6, 8) facing SE, shields 0 (20 next turn), hull 37. Raider: (9, 9) facing W, shields 0, hull 37, 3 squares away.

Shoot, then duck. Firing at any moment of the turn lets the Gunner take the shot while the line is open and the Pilot move to cover afterwards. Next round your shields come back and the raider's don't.

What to take from it

  • Range is the whole fight. A laser reaches 12, a railgun only 6. For two rounds the raider could not shoot back, and everything it did was spent getting close. Know the other ship's gun before you split power.
  • Every Engine point is a square. Speed 0 to 3 means one point in Engines is a third of your speed. The Engineer decided the chase in round 1 and paid for it in round 2.
  • The Gunner picks the moment. In round 1 the Gunner fired from the first square of the move, past the rock. In round 3 the Gunner fired before the move and the Pilot ducked behind the rock afterwards. Call your path out loud and agree where to shoot from.
  • Cover is real. A rock in the line stops beams and railguns from every square behind it. Only missiles route round it, and only a planet stops those.
  • Kinetics want you still. Moving last turn put you at AC 14 against the railgun. It still hit once: moving helps, it doesn't make you safe.
  • Shields first when they can reach you. Shields-down was free at 10 squares and cost 13 hull at 6.

After three rounds. You: (6, 8) behind the asteroid, shields coming back to 5 next turn, hull 37. Raider: (9, 9), 3 squares off, shields 0 and nothing powering them, hull 37. Round 4 is a knife fight at Close range, and only one ship has shields.
